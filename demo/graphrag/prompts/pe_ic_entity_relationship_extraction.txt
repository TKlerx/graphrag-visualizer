-Goal-
Given a text document that is potentially relevant to private equity investment committee preparation and a list of entity types, identify all entities of those types from the text and all relationships among the identified entities.

Use the extraction to support an IC memo that combines target-company facts, sponsor knowledge, market context, transaction context, risks, value creation levers, ESG considerations, and diligence gaps.

-Steps-
1. Identify all entities. For each identified entity, extract the following information:
- entity_name: Name of the entity, capitalized
- entity_type: One of the following types: [{entity_types}]
- entity_description: Comprehensive description of the entity's attributes and activities, emphasizing why the entity matters for investment underwriting
Format each entity as ("entity"<|><entity_name><|><entity_type><|><entity_description>)

2. From the entities identified in step 1, identify all pairs of (source_entity, target_entity) that are clearly related to each other.
For each pair of related entities, extract the following information:
- source_entity: name of the source entity, as identified in step 1
- target_entity: name of the target entity, as identified in step 1
- relationship_description: explanation of how the two entities are related and why that relationship matters for IC diligence
- relationship_strength: a numeric score from 1 to 10 indicating strength of the relationship
Format each relationship as ("relationship"<|><source_entity><|><target_entity><|><relationship_description><|><relationship_strength>)

3. Prefer specific named entities over generic concepts.

4. Capture transaction facts, dates, values, parties, markets, products, customer segments, financial metrics, risks, ESG topics, and value creation levers when present.

5. Mark missing or unresolved evidence as DILIGENCE_GAP only when the text explicitly points to an unanswered question, missing evidence, or required diligence workstream. Do not invent gaps.

6. Do not invent financial metrics that are not present in the source.

7. Extract at most 8 high-signal entities and at most 10 high-signal relationships from each text chunk. Prefer quality over coverage.

8. Do not extract generic entities such as "PRIVATE EQUITY", "MARKET", "CUSTOMERS", or "INVESTORS" unless the text gives a specific named market, customer segment, transaction, or metric that is useful for the IC memo.

9. Return output in English as a single list of all entities and relationships identified in steps 1 and 2. Use **##** as the list delimiter.

10. When finished, output <|COMPLETE|>
