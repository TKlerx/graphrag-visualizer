## 1. Executive Summary

**Draft**: The investment case centers on a European industrial ERP software platform serving small and mid-market manufacturing customers. Public sources indicate Forterro has a portfolio of ERP brands, a large industrial customer base, and a sponsor-backed value creation plan focused on go-to-market expansion, strategic acquisitions, cloud offerings, and operational efficiency.

**Evidence to cite**:

- Partners Group Forterro case study: customer base, industrial ERP positioning, business-building plan.
- Partners Group acquisition announcement: transaction rationale and value creation plan.
- Forterro investor page: subscription-based and cloud-ready strategy.

**Assumptions**:

- Revenue is meaningfully recurring.
- Customer retention is supported by mission-critical workflows.

**Open diligence gaps**:

- ARR, revenue growth, EBITDA margin, gross retention, net retention, customer concentration, leverage, and entry valuation bridge.

**Evidence to cite**:

**Assumptions**:
